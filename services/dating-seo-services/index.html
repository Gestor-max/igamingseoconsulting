Why dating SEO is different

Ads are restricted everywhere

Google Ads, Meta, TikTok — all heavily restrict dating. Organic search is often the only sustainable acquisition channel, which makes it brutally contested.

Big brands own the SERPs

Match Group properties and legacy players dominate. Winning means out-executing on intent-matched pages, topical authority and links — not publishing generic "dating tips" blogs.

Trust is the conversion lever

Nobody joins a dating site they don't trust. Reviews, safety signals, transparent pricing and E-E-A-T signals decide whether organic visitors become members.

Affiliate economics reward precision

CPA and pay-per-lead payouts mean one ranking comparison page can be a five-figure monthly asset. Keyword and intent selection is where the money is won or lost.
