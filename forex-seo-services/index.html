Why forex SEO is different

Google treats forex as YMYL

"Your Money or Your Life" means stricter quality thresholds. Thin affiliate sites and generic content don't rank anymore — expertise, trust and brand signals do.

Brutal competition & ad costs

Paid search in forex is expensive and restricted in many markets. Organic is the only scalable channel — but only if you survive the competitive moat of established brokers.

Regulation shapes SEO

FCA, ESMA, CFTC/NFA and local regulators affect what you can say and target. Your SEO strategy must align keywords, claims and pages with compliance reality.

Trust converts, not traffic

A forex visitor converts into a funded account only after trust is built. SEO for forex means engineering authority at every step — from SERP snippet to deposit page.
